# Reporting a security issue

If you think you have found a security issue in a Pauhu service, website or this repository, please tell us.

## How to report

- Use the contact in `https://pauhu.ai/.well-known/security.txt`.
- Describe the issue, where you found it, and the steps to reproduce it.
- Do not include personal data, passwords or keys that are not yours.

## What we ask

- Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it publicly.
- Test only against accounts and devices you own or are allowed to use.
- Do not access, change or delete other people's data, and do not degrade the service for others.
- Stop and tell us as soon as you reach data that is not yours.

## What we do

- We confirm that we received your report.
- We investigate, fix confirmed issues, and tell you when the fix is out.
- With your permission, we credit you when we disclose the fix.

We do not offer payment for reports.

## Scope

This policy covers Pauhu public services and this repository. The example firewall files in this package are
templates that you adapt and run on your own equipment; issues in how a site applies them are out of scope.
